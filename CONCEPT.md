# Axiomatic — Concept

> *All ideas are possibilities with different probabilities, where the probabilities are combinations and permutations of finite but seemingly infinite things.*
> — the seed thought

## Articulation

**AXIOMATIC** is a causal world engine: a computational model of human civilization capable of evolving forward, tracing backward, and rerunning reality under altered conditions.

At its foundation is the entire human population. Every person conceptually exists, but billions of heavyweight software agents do not. Humanity is represented through extremely cheap computational units—cells, pixels, vectors, distributions or procedural entities. A single unit may represent one person or thousands, depending on how much resolution that part of the world currently requires. Individual characteristics can be generated deterministically when needed rather than permanently stored.

Each unit represents some combination of human attributes and circumstances: age, location, knowledge, ability, education, health, wealth, occupation, creativity, ambition, risk appetite, social connections, access to capital, exposure to information, culture, institutions and environment. These are distributions rather than simplistic personality profiles.

People interact with an equally simulated environment: firms, governments, markets, technologies, resources, infrastructure, laws, prices, institutions, geography and other people. From those interactions emerge employment, consumption, investment, businesses, inventions, failures, migration, inequality, scientific progress, economic growth and eventually whole historical trajectories.

Crucially, Axiomatic does not need to simulate every thought made by every human. It computes probabilities across populations and materialises only consequential events. Billions of people may generate trillions of mundane mental events while only a tiny fraction becomes economically meaningful. Computation therefore follows **causal significance rather than population size**.

When something consequential emerges, resolution can increase dynamically. A statistical cell producing an important invention can split until the particular originating individual is materialised. Because that person's characteristics and history derive deterministically from the previous world state, Axiomatic can reconstruct the path that produced them: family circumstances, education, encounters, accumulated knowledge, opportunities, failures, luck and ultimately the idea itself.

The world therefore possesses a continuously expanding **causal graph**. An outcome is not merely recorded; its ancestry is recorded. A company, technology, recession or productivity boom can be traced backward through the interacting decisions and conditions that produced it.

This makes Axiomatic inherently bidirectional.

**Forward causality:** given this world, what consequences emerge?

**Backward causality:** given this consequence, what produced it?

**Counterfactual causality:** if this cause were removed, altered or introduced, what world would have existed instead?

The simulator can therefore be stopped, rewound, forked and replayed. Make energy dramatically cheaper. Give a particular population better education. Remove a regulation. Introduce a technology ten years earlier. Give an overlooked individual access to capital. Prevent a war. Change fertility. Alter interest rates. Then allow the world to evolve again and compare the resulting causal histories.

Repeated simulations make another class of question possible. Instead of observing that X preceded Y once in actual history, Axiomatic can create thousands of comparable worlds in which X exists, disappears, changes magnitude or occurs under different circumstances. The difference between those worlds becomes evidence about causal relationships inside the model.

Its deepest subject is therefore not economics alone.

Economics is the observable bloodstream through which billions of human decisions propagate.

Axiomatic is ultimately attempting to model **how individual potential, ideas, resources, incentives, institutions, chance and human interaction compound into civilization-scale outcomes.**

That produces applications ranging from economic and policy experimentation to innovation research, business strategy, historical counterfactuals, education, scientific exploration and an extraordinary simulation/game. A government could investigate consequences of a policy. A company could introduce a hypothetical product. A researcher could study conditions that produce innovation. An ordinary user could discover what happens when one seemingly insignificant assumption about civilization changes.

And perhaps its most interesting capability would be discovering things nobody explicitly asked it to find: populations containing unrealised potential, ideas that repeatedly fail because of particular bottlenecks, combinations of circumstances that reliably generate breakthroughs, apparently important causes that turn out to matter very little, or tiny variables whose effects compound enormously over decades.

An LLM generates language by learning relationships within language.

**Axiomatic generates and interrogates worlds through relationships of cause and consequence.**

Its primitive is not the token.

Its primitive is the **cause**.

And its output is not merely a prediction.

Its output is a possible history, together with an inspectable explanation of why that history happened.

## Engineering principles

1. **Possibility is combinatorial; only the adjacent possible is computed.** Every idea combines things that already exist. The space of ideas is effectively infinite, but at any moment a region can only reach combinations of what it already knows. Axiomatic evaluates that frontier and nothing else.
2. **The universe seed holds the axioms; the history seed holds chance.** Geography, starting conditions and which combinations of ideas are valuable are fixed by the universe. Luck varies between histories, so the physics of ideas stays the same in every world.
3. **Never simulate individually what the aggregate decides equally well.** Cells hold distributions. An individual is materialised only when a consequential event needs an originator, and is sampled *conditioned on* having produced it.
4. **Recorded vs reconstructed.** Anything computed when it happened is *recorded*: conditions, chance rolls, the ideas combined, interventions. A materialised person's earlier life is *reconstructed*: plausible and consistent with the recorded world, but not observed. The causal graph labels every link with which kind it is.
5. **Determinism and common random numbers.** Every draw is a hash of (seed, time, place, event). Rewinding replays exactly. A fork differs only where a cause differs.
6. **Order in chaos: ensembles, not single runs.** Outcomes that recur across chance seeds are attractors; rare ones were contingent. Interventions are measured on paired worlds and reported with a spread.
7. **Resolution goes where worlds disagree.** *(Planned.)* Split cells near tipping points, where outcomes vary widely across seeds, not only where events have already happened.
8. **Check the model against reality before trusting it.** Stylized facts should emerge without being programmed in: firm sizes following Zipf's law, regional divergence, and ideas getting harder to find.
